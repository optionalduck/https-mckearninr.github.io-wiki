Ryan Michael McKearnin (born December 22, 2000) is an American computer science student and software developer. He is best known for being really smart and handsome.[citation needed]

Early life[edit]

McKearnin was born in Ridgewood, New Jersey, on December 22, 2000. He grew up in Wyckoff, New Jersey, the son of a Jewish mother and a Catholic father. In 2015, he moved to Glen Rock, New Jersey. McKearnin graduated from Ramapo High School in 2019.
